This script shows the sea surface temperature (SST) anomalies and El Niño / La Niña climate cycles in the Equatorial Pacific (Niño 3.4 region) where the data was extracted from a NOAA climate server. NOAA OISST v2 was used as it provides long-term, high-resolution monthly sea surface temperature observations for climate analysis.
Firstly we get the data from the server, then we sort latitude coordinates (sortby(lat)) and slice the lat (-5 to 5) and lon (190 to 240) of our proposed region.
We apply cosine latitude-weighting (weighted(weights)) to calculate spatial averages and compute monthly anomalies (groupby('time.month')) by subtracting long-term monthly baselines to isolate climate signals from seasonal cycles.
We use plt.plot to display the multi-decadal time-series (teal) and fill_between to shade warm El Niño phases in red (crimson) and cold La Niña phases in blue (dodgerblue).
Finally we lock the reference baseline with axhline(0) and set phase threshold limits (>= 0.5 and <= -0.5) to easily identify historical climate anomaly events across decades
